The 3-Step Ritual

How to Use for Best Results

Follow this relaxing 3-step Ayurvedic ritual 2 to 3 times a week to experience optimal hair density and scalp health.

1

Apply to Scalp

Take a generous amount (10–15ml) of Nature's 18 oil into your palm or apply directly to your scalp section by section using the dropper.

2

Massage 5–10 Mins

Gently massage with your fingertips in circular motions. This stimulates blood circulation and drives the 18 active herbs deep into the roots.

3

Leave & Wash

Leave the oil on for at least 2 to 3 hours (or overnight for intense conditioning). Wash thoroughly with a mild, sulfate-free shampoo.

Pro Hair-Care Tip: For extra absorption, wrap your hair in a warm damp towel for 10 minutes after massaging. Apply consistently 3 times a week for at least 4 weeks for maximum transformation.
